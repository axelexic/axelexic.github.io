This post is a conceptual tour of Brakerski-Gentry-Vaikuntanathan (BGV)
FHE scheme [^BGV12] and its adaptation to Ring-LWE settings by Fan and
Vercauteren [^FV12]. <!--more-->

[^BGV12]: **Z. Brakerski**, **C. Gentry**, and **V. Vaikuntanathan**,
    "(Leveled) Fully Homomorphic Encryption without Bootstrapping," in
    ITCS 2012: Proceedings of the 3rd Innovations in Theoretical
    Computer Science Conference. [Online
    access](https://dl.acm.org/doi/epdf/10.1145/2090236.2090262){:target="_blank"}.

[^FV12]: **J. Fan** and **F. Vercauteren**, "Somewhat Practical Fully
    Homomorphic Encryption." [ePrint
    2012/144](https://eprint.iacr.org/2012/144){:target="_blank"}.

[^GSW13]: **C. Gentry**, **A. Sahai**, and **B. Waters**, in
      "Homomorphic Encryption from Learning with Errors:
      Conceptually-Simpler, Asymptotically-Faster, Attribute-Based", in
      Crypto 2013. [ePrint
      2013/340](https://eprint.iacr.org/2013/340){:target="_blank"}.
