In 2013, Gentry, Sahai, and Waters (GSW) came up with a very elegant FHE
scheme [^GSW13] that's based on the hardness of LWE assumption. A
fundamental structural problem with LWE samples is that algebraically it
only has an additive structure, and defining multiplication is usually a
hassle. As we saw in , gets around this problem
by defining multiplication as tensor product of two LWE samples.

[^GSW13]: **C. Gentry**, **A. Sahai**, and **B. Waters**, in
      "Homomorphic Encryption from Learning with Errors:
      Conceptually-Simpler, Asymptotically-Faster, Attribute-Based", in
      Crypto 2013. [ePrint
      2013/340](https://eprint.iacr.org/2013/340){:target="_blank"}.
