In 2013, Gentry, Sahai, and Waters (GSW) came up with a very elegant FHE
scheme [^GSW13] that's based on the hardness of LWE assumption. A
fundamental structural problem with LWE samples is that "natively" it
only has an additive group structure. Defining multiplication over a set
algebraically is only a group and not a ring often feels unnatural and
is often a hassle. [BGV]({% post_url 2020-09-05-BGV %}), for example,
gets around this problem by defining multiplication as the tensor
product of two LWE samples.

[^GSW13]: **C. Gentry**, **A. Sahai**, and **B. Waters**, in
      "Homomorphic Encryption from Learning with Errors:
      Conceptually-Simpler, Asymptotically-Faster, Attribute-Based", in
      Crypto 2013. [ePrint
      2013/340](https://eprint.iacr.org/2013/340){:target="_blank"}.
